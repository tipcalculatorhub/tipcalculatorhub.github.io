How this tool works

Enter your numbers in the boxes above and press the button. The calculator works entirely in your browser, so your numbers are never sent anywhere. The result appears straight away, and you can change any input and calculate again as often as you like.

Always check that your inputs use the units the labels ask for, and round only at the end. If the answer looks far from what you expected, re-read the inputs before trusting it. For a fuller explanation, including a worked example with real numbers, read the guides linked below.
