Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

A common guideline in many countries is 15 to 20 percent for good service, though customs vary by country.

Should I tip on the tax?

Most guides suggest tipping on the pre-tax amount, since tax is not part of the service.

How do I split a bill fairly?

Split evenly when everyone ordered similar amounts, or by what each person had when orders differ a lot.

What if a service charge is already included?

Check the bill first. If a service charge is already added, an extra tip is optional, not expected.

Should I tip on takeaway?

It is not usually expected, though a small tip for a large or complicated order is a kind gesture.

Is cash better than card for tips?

Cash usually reaches staff faster and in full, while card tips may be pooled or delayed.

Is the calculator free?

Yes, every tool on this site is free and runs in your browser with no sign-up.

Do you store my bill?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.
